jul 2026 — nowindependent · manuscript in prep

Compositional generalisation in LLMs — a diagnostic on HotMoE (AAAI 2026)

Do models that master every skill also master their compositions? Often: exactly no.

  • The compositional collapse survives scale. Five deterministic text-transform skills with program-checkable ground truth, swept 0.5B → 7B across two model families (three seeds, frozen predictions). At 7B the atomic skills saturate, yet many held-out compositions sit at exactly 0.000 while the same checkpoint executes each step perfectly in isolation; a dense-LoRA control reproduces the pattern.
  • Traced to generation order, then reversed. A matched intervention flips composition from 0.68 to 0.000; supervising an explicit intermediate state revives the dead cells (0.000 → 0.975), replicating on Mistral-7B.

jul 2026 — nowindependent

Distribution-level fidelity of LLM-simulated L2 dialogue

A question Gao et al. (ACL 2025) leave open: are L1-conditioned simulated learner populations distinguishable at all?

  • MMD permutation tests on dialogue-level embeddings (from-scratch DUAL reimplementation), seven L1s × four LLMs; a native-speaker control separates from every L1 (28/28) at empirical Type-I 0.050.
  • Model identity dominates the simulated L1 — within GPT-4o and DeepSeek-V3 no L1 pair is distinguishable after Bonferroni (0/21 each), while the same L1 across models differs in 24/42 cases with 2–5× larger MMD².

Training-free financial misinformation detection (CATO-V)

Beat the fine-tuned state of the art without training a thing.

  • A DeepSeek V4 pipeline — hybrid retrieval, self-consistency voting, disagreement arbitration, sufficiency-gated abstention — reaches 77.2% accuracy / 0.745 macro-F1 on the 1,304-claim Fin-Fact test set, above fine-tuned FMDLlama3 on all eight metrics, in 3.7 minutes at $0.39.
  • Recovered the hidden test labels to evaluate honestly — matched all 1,953 training claims to sources, recovered all 1,304 held-out labels, documented the protocol.

oct 2025 — nowMetriful · independent

Portion-size bias in vision-language nutrition estimation

Diagnosed a bias that flips sign with portion size, then fixed it with depth.

  • Across five public benchmarks (~1,300 weighed dishes), Gemini vision models over-estimate small portions and under-estimate large ones by up to 34%.
  • A depth-grounded corrector roughly halves median calorie error (51% → 26%) under a sub-5-second budget — where monocular relative depth cannot substitute. Ships in Metriful.
